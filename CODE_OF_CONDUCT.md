# 📜 Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We, as members, contributors, and maintainers of the **NFSFU234FormValidation Official Documentation Website**, pledge to make participation in our community a welcoming, respectful, harassment-free, and enjoyable experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We are committed to fostering an open, inclusive, and collaborative environment where developers of all backgrounds can learn, contribute, and grow together.

---

## 🌍 Our Standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment include:

* ❤️ Being respectful and considerate.
* 🤝 Welcoming new contributors and helping them learn.
* 💬 Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
* 📚 Sharing knowledge openly.
* 🌱 Being patient with people who are still learning.
* 🎉 Celebrating the success of others.
* 💡 Focusing on what is best for the community and the project.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour include:

* Harassment, discrimination, or hateful language.
* Personal attacks or insults.
* Trolling or deliberately disruptive behaviour.
* Publishing another person's private information without permission.
* Repeatedly ignoring project guidelines after feedback.
* Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional community.

---

## 🛡️ Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, pull requests, issues, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct and may take appropriate action in response to unacceptable behaviour.

---

## 🌐 Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including but not limited to:

* GitHub Issues
* GitHub Discussions
* Pull Requests
* Documentation contributions
* Community discussions
* Any other official communication channels related to this project

It also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

---

## 🚨 Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness behaviour that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it to the project maintainers.

When reporting an incident, please include as much relevant information as possible so it can be investigated fairly and confidentially.

All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with respect for the privacy and safety of everyone involved.

---

## ⚖️ Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers may take actions including, but not limited to:

* Friendly clarification or guidance.
* Private warning.
* Temporary restriction from community interactions.
* Permanent removal from the community for serious or repeated violations.

The appropriate response will depend on the severity and context of the behaviour.
